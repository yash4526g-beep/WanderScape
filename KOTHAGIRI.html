Bike Ride at Ooty

A bike ride to Kotagiri is a scenic escape into the heart of the Nilgiris. Situated at around 1,800 meters above sea level, Kotagiri is one of the oldest hill stations in Tamil Nadu and is known for its pleasant weather year-round. The ride takes you through winding mountain roads flanked by lush tea estates, pine forests, and occasional waterfalls. Gentle curves and long stretches of misty roads make it a rider’s paradise. Popular stops like Catherine Falls, Elk Falls, and Kodanad View Point offer stunning vistas worth every halt. With its calm atmosphere and green landscapes, Kotagiri offers the perfect mix of adventure and serenity for bikers who love riding into nature’s lap....
