Bike Ride at Yercaud

A bike ride to Yercaud is a refreshing escape into the Eastern Ghats. Perched at around 1,500 meters above sea level in Tamil Nadu’s Shevaroy Hills, Yercaud offers riders a blend of smooth ghat roads, sharp hairpin bends, and panoramic valley views. The route is lined with coffee plantations, spice gardens, and dense forests, filling the air with a rich, earthy aroma. Scenic stops like Yercaud Lake, Pagoda Point, and the 32-km Loop Road make the journey as enjoyable as the destination. With its cool climate, winding roads, and serene landscapes, Yercaud delivers the perfect balance of adventure and relaxation for biking enthusiasts..
