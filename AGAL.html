Bike Ride at Agali, Kerala

Agali is a peaceful hill destination nestled in the scenic landscapes of Kerala’s Palakkad district. Surrounded by misty mountains, lush forests, and winding roads, the region offers a refreshing escape into nature. The quiet villages, green valleys, and cool mountain air create a calm and relaxing atmosphere for visitors. The journey through the hills offers beautiful views of the Western Ghats and the untouched charm of rural Kerala. Visitors can enjoy the natural beauty while experiencing the simple and peaceful lifestyle of the surrounding communities. It is a perfect place to slow down, explore the mountains, and discover a quieter side of Kerala.
