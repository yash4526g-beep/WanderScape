Bike Ride at Dhondenling Tibetan Settlement

Dhondenling Tibetan Settlement is a peaceful retreat nestled in the scenic hills of Karnataka. The settlement is home to Tibetan communities who have preserved their rich culture, traditions, and way of life. Colorful prayer flags, monasteries, and peaceful surroundings create a unique atmosphere for visitors. The gentle hills and cool climate make it a refreshing escape from the busy city life. Visitors can experience Tibetan culture while enjoying the calm beauty of the surrounding landscape. It is a perfect place to slow down, explore, and experience a different side of the hills....
