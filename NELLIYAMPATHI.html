NELLIAMPATHI

"Where the road meets the clouds"

Journey

Bike Ride at Nelliampathi, Kerala A bike ride to Nelliampathi is an unforgettable blend of thrill and tranquility. Located in Kerala’s Palakkad district, this hill station sits at around 1,600 meters above sea level, offering mist-covered roads, dense forests, and lush tea and coffee plantations. The journey begins with a series of sharp hairpin bends, each opening to breathtaking valley views and cool mountain air. Riders often stop at viewpoints like Seethargundu and Kesavanpara to soak in panoramic landscapes. The winding ghat roads, changing weather, and occasional wildlife sightings make the ride both exciting and peaceful. Nelliampathi is not just a destination—it’s the kind of road trip where the journey is as memorable as the place itself.
